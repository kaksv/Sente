### Applying the palette

Sente currently uses hand-written CSS rather than Tailwind or shadcn, so these
colors are documented here and are not yet applied to `app/globals.css`. For a
future theme pass, map `bg-base` to the page background, `bg-elevated` to
panels, `primary` to key actions and focus rings, and `fg-base` to primary text.
Keep the existing red, green, and amber status colors distinct from the brand
primary.

## Typography — Inter + JetBrains Mono

- **Display + body:** Inter
- **Mono (numbers, timestamps, message text):** JetBrains Mono

The pairing uses `next/font/google` in `app/layout.tsx` with the CSS variables
`--font-sans` and `--font-mono`. It is the default pairing because no typography
choice was submitted after the preview.

### Type guidance

- Page title: 21–24 px, semibold
- Section labels: 11–13 px, uppercase only for concise labels
- Body and explanations: 13–15 px with comfortable line height
- Balances, timestamps, and SMS: monospace with tabular numbers

## Gradients

Not used. Keep the interface restrained and let the ledger data carry the
hierarchy.

## Tone and voice

Use measured, patient, specific language. Explain the amount and the signal
behind each flag. Describe a risk as a reason to review, not proof of fraud.

Avoid hype, urgency, unsupported certainty, and jargon. Prefer direct wording
such as “Balance does not match the previous message” over vague labels.

> A mismatch is a reason to review—not proof of fraud.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do**

- Use the palette tokens as the source for future theme changes.
- Keep error, success, and warning colors semantically distinct.
- Use monospace for financial figures and SMS text.
- Preserve the “Risk score, not proof” caveat.

**Don't**

- Claim that a payment is definitely fraudulent.
- Use color alone to communicate a verdict.
- Add urgency cues or decorative gradients to financial alerts.
